### Quiz Time! 🧠

**Question 5:**

Who was **Veer Savarkar**?

*(Hint: He was a fearless freedom fighter, writer, and thinker.)*

---

### Answer! ✅

**Vinayak Damodar Savarkar**, known as Veer Savarkar, was a brave freedom fighter, activist, and writer.

He spent many years imprisoned by the British for his revolutionary activities, including time in the Cellular Jail in the Andaman Islands. He strongly believed in India's independence and cultural strength.

---

### Quiz Time! 🧠

**Question 9:**

Who was **Rani Laxmi Bai**?

*(Hint: The brave queen of Jhansi!)*

![Rani Laxmi Bai](placeholder_laxmibai.png) <!-- Replace with actual image URL -->

---

### Answer! ✅

**Rani Laxmi Bai** was the fearless queen of the princely state of Jhansi.

She fought valiantly against the British during the 1857 uprising, riding her horse into battle with her son tied to her back. "Khoob Ladi Mardani, Woh Toh Jhansi Wali Rani Thi!"

---

### Quiz Time! 🧠

**Question 10:**

Who were the **Sahibzade** (sons of Guru Gobind Singh Ji)?

*(Hint: Young heroes who sacrificed everything for their faith!)*

![Golden Temple](placeholder_goldentemple.png) <!-- Replace with actual image URL -->

---

### Answer! ✅

The **Sahibzade** were the four brave sons of Guru Gobind Singh Ji:
*   Baba Ajit Singh Ji
*   Baba Jujhar Singh Ji
*   Baba Zorawar Singh Ji
*   Baba Fateh Singh Ji

They showed incredible courage and faith, with the younger two choosing to be bricked alive rather than give up their beliefs.

### Quiz Time! 🧠

**Question 13:**

Who was **Sir C. V. Raman**?

*(Hint: He won a Nobel Prize for physics!)* ✨

![CV Raman Effect](placeholder_raman_effect.png) <!-- Replace with actual image URL -->

---

### Answer! ✅

**Sir C. V. Raman** was a famous Indian physicist who won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930.

He discovered why light changes color when it passes through different materials (the Raman Effect).
